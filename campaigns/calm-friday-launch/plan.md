## 1. Campaign overview

**Campaign name:** The Calm Friday: Your Spot Is Ready

**Summary:** A four-week, email-only launch that invites our 2,000 waitlist members to join The Calm Friday, a **free membership**. Members earn credits when they swap or sell gadgets to us, and get 15% off on Calm Friday, Friday 1 January 2027. Waitlist members who join by Thursday 31 December also get 100 bonus credits. The tone stays calm, with no pressure, to match our brand.

**Primary goal:** 300 waitlist members join The Calm Friday between Monday 7 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027.

**Secondary goals:**
- Teach every waitlist member how The Calm Friday works (joining, swap credits, the Calm Friday discount).
- Get at least 30 Past Sellers to swap or sell an item during the campaign.
- Turn new members into Calm Friday shoppers on 1 January.
- Learn which segment responds best, to guide the next campaign.

### The offer at a glance

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| Membership | Free. Nothing to pay. |
| Swap credits | **Up to 500 credits** (up to Rs 5,000) per accepted item, based on the item and its condition |
| Credit value | 100 credits = Rs 1,000 |
| Credit expiry | Credits do not expire while the account is active. They are lost only if the account is deactivated. |
| Waitlist thank-you | **100 bonus credits** (Rs 1,000) for joining by **Thu 31 Dec 2026** |
| Calm Friday | **Fri 1 Jan 2027**, one day only |
| 15% off applies to | Phones, laptops, tablets and accessories |
| 15% off does not apply to | Audio and wearables (earbuds, headphones, speakers, smartwatches) |
| Combining | Credits can be used on the same order as the 15% off |
| Quality rule | We accept items of decent to good quality only |

300 out of 2,000 is 15%. Because joining is free and these people asked to hear from us, this is a realistic target. If week 1 goes well, a stretch target of 400 is worth watching for. The two things that matter most are a clear offer and a sign-up page that takes under a minute.

## 2. Target audience

About 2,000 people who joined the waitlist. All of them gave us their email on purpose, so they are warm. They split into three groups (sizes are estimates **[confirm]**).

### Past Buyers (primary, about 1,000)

People who have bought a pre-loved gadget from EasyBuy before.

- **Want:** good gadgets that are kind to their wallet; no surprises about condition.
- **Worry:** "It's free, so what's the catch?" and "Will I actually save anything?"
- **Buying stage:** decision. They trust us already. They need to see the saving in rupees on 1 Jan.

### Past Sellers (primary, about 500)

People who have sold or swapped an item with us before.

- **Want:** a fair, easy way to turn old gadgets into something useful.
- **Worry:** "How many credits will my item actually get? Will it be accepted?"
- **Buying stage:** decision. Swap credits are the strongest hook for them, as long as we are honest that it is "up to" 500.

### Quiet Users (secondary, about 500)

People who joined the waitlist but have not bought or sold with us.

- **Want:** to understand what EasyBuy is and whether pre-loved is right for them.
- **Worry:** quality, and whether pre-loved gadgets are worth it.
- **Buying stage:** consideration. They need trust before they need an offer.

**Where they spend time:** their email inbox (our only channel for this campaign) and the EasyBuy website, where the sign-up page lives.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** The Calm Friday is a free membership for a calmer way to buy and swap pre-loved gadgets. As a waitlist member, your spot is ready, with 100 bonus credits if you join by 31 December.

| # | Supporting message | Proof point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Free to join.** Nothing to pay, no catch. | Membership is free. Say plainly what it includes. |
| 2 | **Good for your wallet.** Members save on Calm Friday. | 15% off phones, laptops, tablets and accessories on Fri 1 Jan. Credits can be used on the same order. |
| 3 | **Your old gadgets are worth something.** Swap or sell an item and earn credits. | Up to 500 credits (up to Rs 5,000) per accepted item, based on its value. Credits don't expire while your account is active. |
| 4 | **Quality you can trust.** We only accept items of decent to good quality, and we describe each one honestly. | Our item check process. Show what we check; do not promise perfection. |
| 5 | **No rush, ever.** Take your time. | Credits don't expire. Even the bonus deadline is mentioned calmly, never as pressure. |

### Message by segment

- **Past Buyers:** "You already know pre-loved works. Join free, and save 15% on your next phone, laptop or tablet on Calm Friday." Lead with messages 1 and 2.
- **Past Sellers:** "Your old gadgets can earn up to 500 credits, and credits never run out while your account is active." Lead with message 3.
- **Quiet Users:** "No pressure. Here is who we are, how we check every item, and how The Calm Friday works." Lead with messages 4 and 5.

### Accuracy rules for every email

- Always say **"up to 500 credits"**. Never "500 credits" on its own.
- When mentioning the 15%, name the categories: "phones, laptops, tablets and accessories", in that order and with that wording. Never say "15% off everything". In a preview line, if all four do not fit, write "phones, laptops, tablets and more".
- When saying credits don't expire, add "while your account is active" at least once per email (or link to the Q&A).
- The bonus is **100 credits for joining by Thursday 31 December**. Use the same wording everywhere.
- Call it a **membership**, never a subscription.

### Voice checklist for every email

- Open with **Dear [name],** and close with **Regards, The EasyBuy Team**.
- Say pre-loved (or twice-loved, pre-adored, secondhandsome). Never the words on the avoid list in `brand/voice.md`.
- No urgency words, even near the deadline. We remind, we do not push.
- None of the claims in `brand/voice.md` section 4. Describe condition honestly instead.
- Short sentences, simple words, one clear button per email.

## 4. Channel strategy

### Email (the only channel)

- **Why it fits:** every waitlist member gave us their email and expects to hear from us. It costs almost nothing, and we can tailor each email to the segment.
- **Format:** short, mostly-text emails with one button. Plain layout, logo at the top, no heavy images (they load slowly and look less personal).
- **Effort:** medium. About 9 campaign emails, with segment or member versions of 3 of them, plus a welcome email.
- **Budget:** all of it. See section 8.

### Two tracks

- **Join track** (waitlist members who have not joined yet): E1 to E8, asking them to join.
- **Member track** (anyone who has joined): the welcome email, then the member versions of E7 and E8 about Calm Friday. Members stop getting join emails as soon as they join.
- **Email files for E7:** `emails/04-calm-friday-is-tomorrow.md` is the join-track version. `emails/04-calm-friday-is-tomorrow-members.md` is the member-track version.

### Supporting pieces (not channels, but needed)

- **Sign-up page** on the website. Every join-track email links here. Must be live before the first send.
- **Welcome email**, sent automatically when someone joins. Confirms the 100 bonus credits (if joined by 31 Dec) and the Calm Friday date.

We are not using social, ads or partners in this campaign. If the target looks out of reach after week 2, a short post on our own social pages is the cheapest extra step (see Risks).

### Sending rules

- Anyone who joins stops getting join-track emails at once and gets the welcome email instead. They move to the member track (E7 and E8 member versions).
- Anyone who unsubscribes is removed at once.
- **Quiet Users get fewer emails:** E1, E2, E3, E4, E7 and E8 only. Fewer emails to less active people protects our sender reputation.
- Stop emailing any Quiet User who has not opened anything by E4.
- If unsubscribes are above 0.5% per email by 18 Dec, drop E5.
- Send in the morning, local time.
- Most people get 6 to 9 emails over 4 weeks, about 2 a week, with two close together on 31 Dec and 1 Jan.

## 9. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | What could happen | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **"Up to 500" disappoints** | People expect 500 credits for any item and feel let down when they get less. | Always say "up to". Give real examples in E2, E4 and on the sign-up page. |
| **15% confusion** | People expect 15% off earbuds or smartwatches and feel misled on 1 Jan. | Name the three categories every time. Say plainly in E4 that audio and wearables are not included. |
| **"Free" feels like a catch** | People wonder what they are signing up for. | Say exactly what membership includes and that there is nothing to pay, in E1 and E4. |
| **Calm Friday is New Year's Day** | People are away or busy on 1 Jan and miss it. | Remind on 31 Dec, keep the 1 Jan email very short, and keep the 15% on all day. |
| **The target is missed** | Week 1 comes in well under 120. | If under 90 by 13 Dec: rewrite E3 around the strongest segment, add a second resend to non-openers, and consider one post on our own social pages. |
| **Holiday timing** | Weeks 3 and 4 fall over Christmas and New Year, when inboxes are busy. | Lighter week 3, no sends 24 to 27 Dec, fresh-start angle for week 4. Plan for most joins in weeks 1 and 2. |
| **Quiet Users hurt our sender reputation** | Old or unused addresses bounce or mark us as spam. | Remove bounced addresses before launch, send Quiet Users fewer emails, and stop emailing any who have not opened by E4. |
| **Pressure creeps into the copy** | The bonus deadline on 31 Dec tempts urgency words, which go against our voice. | Run `/marketing:brand-review` on every email, and twice on E6 and E7. |
| **Quality claims go too far** | Saying items are perfect breaks trust and our claims rules. | Talk about our item check and honest descriptions, never about perfect condition. |
| **Store set-up fails** | Bonus credits don't appear, or the 15% applies to the wrong items. | Test both before launch and again on 31 Dec. |
