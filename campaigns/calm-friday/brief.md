# Calm Friday Swap Campaign: Brief

Working name only. The campaign does not have a confirmed name yet.

## Who it is for

Current EasyBuy users.

## The offer

- A user swaps an item, or sells it directly to EasyBuy, and earns **500 credits**.
- Credits go towards pre-loved items in the store.
- **100 credits = 1,000 rupees**, so 500 credits = 5,000 rupees.
- On **Calm Friday**, EasyBuy gives **15% off**.

## Rules that still apply

- We accept an item only when it is of decent to good quality.
- Voice, words and claims follow `brand/voice.md`.
- Planning and drafts only. No email goes to real people.

## Decisions (confirmed 2026-11-03)

- **What The Calm Friday is:** a **free membership**, not a paid subscription. Waitlist members join for free, and joining unlocks swap credits and the Calm Friday discount. No price, no billing.
- **Swap credits:** **up to 500 credits** per accepted item, based on the item's value. Emails must say "up to 500 credits", never "500 credits" on its own.
- **Credit expiry:** credits do not expire. They are lost only if the account is deactivated.
- **Calm Friday date:** one day only, **Friday 1 January 2027**.
- **15% off applies to:** phones, laptops and tablets, and accessories. It does **not** apply to audio and wearables (earbuds, headphones, speakers, smartwatches).
- **Stacking:** credits can be used on the same order as the 15% off.
- **Items we do not accept (confirmed 2026-11-07):** our quality team checks every item we receive. Items that pass go into our store and earn credits. Items that do not pass are sent back, and the customer pays the return delivery cost. Never promise free returns.
- **Waitlist thank-you:** **100 bonus credits (Rs 1,000)** for members who join by **Thursday 31 December 2026**, so they can use them on Calm Friday.
- **Sending around Calm Friday:** a calm reminder email on Thu 31 Dec, plus one short email on the morning of Fri 1 Jan.
- **Segments:** real numbers not known yet. Working estimate: about 50% Past Buyers, 25% Past Sellers, 25% Quiet Users.
