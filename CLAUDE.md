# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/` - the business's voice, tone, logo notes and style rules.
- `leads/` - contact lists and audience notes (planning only, never for sending).
- `campaigns/` - campaign plans, email sequences and drafts.
- `notes/` - research, decisions and anything else worth keeping.

## Rules

- Write in plain English.
- Never send email to real people. Sending through Resend is allowed only to
  Resend's test addresses (ending in `@resend.dev`) and to my own address
  (the `MY_EMAIL` value in `.claude/settings.local.json`). A guard will enforce this in the next step.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
