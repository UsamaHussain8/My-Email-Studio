# EasyBuy Brand Voice

EasyBuy is an online store selling twice-loved items. We are launching a sale for our users called **The Eid Festivities**, ahead of Eid.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Calm, warm, friendly.**

- We lean towards formal, with a touch of casual conversation.
- We are never bland. A little wordplay keeps us human, especially in coined words like "secondhandsome".
- Keep the English simple. Short, clear words beat clever ones.

## 2. Words we use

- pre-loved
- twice-loved
- pre-adored
- secondhandsome

Use them with a light touch, so a coined word feels like a smile and not a gimmick. Always call our items pre-loved (or one of the words above), never second-hand.

## 3. Words we avoid

- "second-hand" (we say pre-loved instead)
- Pressure and spam words: "last chance", "hurry", "left behind", and anything like them
- Hype words: "insane", "crazy", "epic", "blast", "bomb", "raining" (as in "deals raining"), "dhamaka"
- Tough or fancy English words. If a simpler word works, use it.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- That an item is "brand new"
- That an item is "like new"
- "100% good quality" or any blanket quality guarantee
- That an item is "flawless"
- That an item is from a genuine brand, unless we have checked and can prove it

Describe each item honestly, including its condition and any marks.

## 5. How we sign off

- Greeting: **Dear [name],**
- Closing:

  Regards,
  The EasyBuy Team
