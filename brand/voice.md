# EasyBuy Brand Voice

EasyBuy is an online store where people buy and sell pre-loved electronic items and gadgets, and nothing else. We accept an item only when it is of decent to good quality.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Calm, warm, friendly.**

- We are a bit casual, like a helpful person talking to one customer.
- Keep the English simple. Short, clear words work best.

## 2. Words we use

- pre-loved
- twice-loved
- pre-adored
- secondhandsome
- swap
- wallet

Always call our items pre-loved (or one of the words above), never second-hand.

## 3. Words we avoid

- second-hand
- last chance
- hurry
- insane
- crazy
- epic
- dhamaka
- left behind
- raining
- blast
- bomb
- what are you waiting for?

## 4. Claims we must never make

- brand new
- like new
- flawless
- 100% battery health
- 100% good quality
- totally guaranteed

## 5. How we sign off

- Greeting: **Dear [name],**
- Closing:

  Regards,
  The EasyBuy Team
