# Handoff: Email 01 subject line (Calm Friday launch)

- **Past Buyers:** saving money with no surprises. They want the 15% shown in rupees and proof that "free" has no catch.
- **Past Sellers:** a fair, easy deal for old gadgets. They want honest "up to 500 credits" and to know if their item will be accepted.
- **Quiet Users:** trust and quality. They want to know who we are and how we check items before seeing any offer.
- **Angle A (calm daily ritual):** e.g. "Your spot is ready for your next quiet minute". Very on-brand, but says nothing about value.
- **Angle B (discovering new deals):** e.g. "Your spot is ready: calmer pre-loved deals". Calm, and it points to value.
- **Pick: Angle B, line 1.** It speaks to Past Buyers, our biggest group (about 1,000), and keeps "Your spot is ready" and "calmer".
- It also stays gentle enough for Sellers and Quiet Users. Angle A line 1 works as the E1b resend subject.
